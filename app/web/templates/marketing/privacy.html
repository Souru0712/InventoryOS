Privacy Policy

Draft for review — have a lawyer check this before you take money.

What we collect

  • Account details: your business name, email address and a hashed password.
  • Point-of-sale data: locations, catalog items, inventory counts and order line items, read from the account you connect.
  • Documents you upload: photographs of supplier invoices and delivery notes, and the line items read from them.
  • Usage data: basic logs needed to operate and debug the service.

How we use it

To show you your inventory, detect low stock, send the alerts and reports you configure, and write corrected counts back to your point-of-sale system when you ask. We do not sell your data and we do not use it to build profiles of your customers.

Processors we rely on

Your point-of-sale provider (to read and write your data), our hosting and email providers, our payment processor, and an AI provider used solely to read the invoice photographs you upload. Uploaded documents are sent for that extraction and are not used to train models.

Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. On request we delete your account data, including uploaded documents, within 30 days, except where we must keep records for tax or legal reasons.

Security

Point-of-sale access tokens are encrypted at rest. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Each business's data is isolated and queries are scoped to the signed-in account.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by emailing us.
