Questions we get asked

Do I have to change how I use Square? No. Vontry reads your Square catalog and orders; it never changes your stock in Square.

I don't use a POS. Can I still use it? Yes. Enter each day's sales by hand or import a file, and connect a POS later from the date you choose.

What if I weigh things instead of counting them? That is the point. Stock can be tracked by count, by weight or by volume.
